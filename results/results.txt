EU Health Inequalities and Life Expectancy Analysis

Countries analysed: 27
Complete country-year observations: 270
Years analysed: 2015-2024

Mean life expectancy across country-year observations: 79.97 years
Mean income inequality across country-year observations: 4.81
Unweighted mean country life expectancy in 2015: 79.53 years
Unweighted mean country life expectancy in 2024: 80.89 years
Change in unweighted mean from 2015 to 2024: +1.36 years

Latest-year highest life expectancy (2024): Spain (84.0 years)
Latest-year lowest life expectancy (2024): Bulgaria (75.8 years)

Pearson correlation across pooled country-year observations:
r = -0.428
p = <0.001

Simple linear regression: life_expectancy ~ income_inequality
income_inequality coefficient = -1.065 years per one-point increase in S80/S20
p = <0.001
R-squared = 0.183

Brief interpretation:
Higher S80/S20 values were associated with lower life expectancy in this pooled country-year analysis.
The estimated slope differed from zero at the 5% level in this simple model.
These are unadjusted, observational country-year associations; repeated observations from countries are pooled, so results do not establish causation and should not be treated as an individual-level relationship.
